Frequently Asked Questions

Kuchaman Sports Academy (KSA) offers dedicated facilities for Cricket and Swimming. Cricket facilities feature 3 Regular Cricket Nets (up to 4 players per net) and 1 Big Box Cricket Net accommodating full 11 vs 11 team play.

Each regular cricket net accommodates a maximum of 4 players at one time for focused individual and small-group practice. Our Big Box cricket net supports full 11 vs 11 team gameplay.

Our pricing is simple and affordable: ₹100 per person per hour for facility access.

Big Box Cricket is our large enclosed arena specifically designed for complete team matches, supporting full 11 vs 11 team play with professional netting and turf.

Yes! Kuchaman Sports Academy includes a dedicated swimming facility built for players, sports enthusiasts, and fitness training.

You can easily book your cricket net or swimming session by clicking the "Book Now" button on this website or contacting KSA directly.
